# LATTICCE FILMS — Prompt visual de origen

Estado: dependencia visual de respaldo para generar o corregir frames faltantes. No autoriza modificar ni reinterpretar los keyframes canónicos.

Regla de prioridad:

1. Keyframes canónicos adyacentes.
2. Brief maestro de “Sigue la luz”.
3. Este prompt de origen.

```text
Create a photorealistic cinematic establishing shot for LATTICCE FILMS, preserving the exact architectural language, atmosphere, lighting, materiality and visual identity of the reference image.

FORMAT AND COMPOSITION:
4K UHD horizontal image, 3840 × 2160 pixels, 16:9 cinematic aspect ratio, specifically composed for a full-width website screen. No vertical composition. No cropping that makes the architecture feel close. This is the FIRST and WIDEST shot of a progressive cinematic sequence.

SCENE:
A vast, desolate open landscape under an enormous overcast storm sky. In the far distance, perfectly centered on the horizon, stands a monumental brutalist concrete structure inspired by the joined double-T geometry of the LATTICCE identity.

The architecture must NOT look like a logo placed into the environment. It must feel like a real, structurally plausible monumental construction whose architectural proportions naturally evoke two connected capital T forms.

The building consists of two massive vertical concrete volumes supporting a single dominant horizontal upper volume. Preserve the characteristic slight architectural relief and depth of the upper horizontal beam: it must project subtly forward and backward, revealing its thickness, underside, lateral faces and structural mass. It must never appear like a flat rectangular graphic.

The geometry should remain extremely simple, severe and monumental.

At this distance, do NOT reveal additional buildings, wings, decorations or temple structures behind it. The viewer should initially perceive only this mysterious monumental TT-shaped gateway standing alone in the landscape.

CAMERA:
Extremely wide establishing shot.
Camera positioned very far away from the structure.
Low-to-medium camera height, approximately human eye level but subtly lowered to increase monumentality.
Perfect frontal symmetry.
Strong central vanishing point.
The structure occupies only a relatively small portion of the center of the frame.

The overwhelming majority of the composition is environment: enormous sky, distant horizon and expansive wet ground.

A tiny human silhouette may be visible directly on the central axis, approaching the structure from a great distance. The person must be extremely small, almost imperceptible, functioning primarily as a reference for scale.

LIGHTING:
Preserve the cold monochromatic lighting of the reference.

Near-black concrete.
Deep charcoal shadows.
Neutral gray atmosphere.
Soft silver-gray highlights.
Absolutely no blue, purple, gold, orange or warm tones.

The sky is heavily overcast with dense volumetric storm clouds, but the lighting remains soft and photographic rather than theatrical.

A powerful diffused white light exists behind and through the narrow central opening of the structure. It should not resemble artificial neon or science-fiction illumination. It feels like natural atmospheric daylight penetrating through fog.

The distant white opening becomes a subtle visual destination.

ENVIRONMENT:
The foreground is an enormous dark wet concrete or stone plain after rain.

Its surface is irregular, physically realistic and subtly cracked, with shallow puddles and natural imperfections.

The moisture creates a long, restrained reflection of the distant white opening extending toward the camera.

Sparse fog and atmospheric haze soften the horizon.

No vegetation should dominate the image.
No city.
No futuristic infrastructure.
No additional objects.
No vehicles.
No signage.
No text.
No symbols.
No visible LATTICCE logo.

VISUAL LANGUAGE:
Brutalist architecture photographed as serious auteur cinema.

Monumental but restrained.
Cold.
Silent.
Oppressive.
Contemplative.
Mysterious.
Architecturally believable.

The image should feel like a frame extracted from a high-budget arthouse science-fiction film rather than architectural concept art.

Photorealistic concrete texture, subtle weathering, physically correct scale, realistic atmospheric perspective, cinematic dynamic range, fine natural film grain, deep blacks without crushed detail, controlled highlights, realistic volumetric fog, extremely detailed surface reflections.

The composition should communicate one simple idea:

the character has discovered something monumental in the distance and is beginning the journey toward it.

This frame must leave substantial visual and spatial progression available for the following shots, where the camera will gradually approach the structure and eventually end behind the character in an over-the-shoulder shot.

4K UHD, 3840 × 2160, 16:9, horizontal cinematic frame, website hero quality, photorealistic, extremely high architectural detail.
```
